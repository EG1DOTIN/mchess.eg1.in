Information Collection And Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide, protect, and optimize our Service to you.

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data
Marwadi Chess is an open educational and interactive portal and does not require user account registration, logins, or contact form submissions. If you choose to contact us directly via our contact page, we use your submitted inquiry solely to respond to your request.

Website Analytics & Usage Data
When you access our Service, we automatically collect non-invasive telemetry and usage data ("Usage Data") to understand website traffic patterns and optimize user experience. This includes:

  • Device & Browser Information: Operating system (e.g., Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, Linux, ChromeOS), browser type, browser version, preferred language, and screen resolution.
  • Network & Approximate Location: Public Internet Protocol (IP) address, approximate geographic location (city, region, country, postal area), and Internet Service Provider (ISP) / Autonomous System (Org). We do not collect precise GPS coordinates.
  • Browsing Activity: Pages visited within our website, landing page, referring website, and timestamp of the visit.
  • Session Identifiers: Anonymous, temporary session tokens stored in browser sessionStorage to prevent duplicate traffic logging during a single browsing session.

No Sale of Data Guarantee
We strictly respect your privacy. We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade visitor analytics or personal data with any third-party advertisers, brokers, or external entities.

Tracking & Storage Technologies
We use browser sessionStorage and localStorage to manage sessions, remember user preferences (such as chessboard settings and themes), and securely operate our Service. Unlike tracking cookies, our session storage does not follow you across third-party websites.

Analytics Notice & Opt-Out Choice
When you visit our website, a notice is presented informing you: "We collect anonymous information to optimize tools/Apps performance." with prominent [Decline] and [OK] buttons. Clicking Decline immediately halts telemetry collection and stores an opt-out flag in your browser's localStorage so no tracking occurs during future visits. You can revisit your choice at any time by clicking Analytics Preferences.

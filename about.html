About Marwadi Chess

Marwadi Chess (मारवाड़ी शतरंज) is a modern, open-source, and community-driven chess platform built for chess lovers, learners, and masters around the world. What began as a heartfelt passion project has evolved into a comprehensive digital destination for strategic chess training, interactive online arenas, tactical puzzles, live master broadcasts, and timeless chess philosophy.

🏛️ Celebrating 4,500 Years of Chess Heritage

Chess is one of humanity’s greatest intellectual treasures, born in ancient India over 4,500 years ago as Chaturanga and Ashtapada during the Indus Valley era. From ancient carved stone tablets to modern digital battlefields, the royal game of strategy continues to inspire thinkers across generations.

Marwadi Chess honors this rich cultural heritage by blending time-tested chess wisdom with cutting-edge digital training tools.
